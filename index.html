The studio

We design the surfaces your market already trusts.

Fixfie began as a two-person craft practice inside a SoHo loft, working after hours for fintech founders who needed more than a template. Today we are a compact atelier of designers, writers, and technologists who still treat every engagement as a signature piece.

We do not chase volume. We take a limited number of retainers each year so partners stay on the work, pixels stay considered, and delivery stays calm even when the brief is not.
